History and Core concepts

Modern football evolved in England during the 19th century and quickly became a global phenomenon. Achieving success requires a balance between disciplined defense and fast attacking transitions. Mastering tactical positioning allows teams to control the tempo of every match. Today, millions of players and fans participate in organized leagues worldwide.

Key Dates

  1. 1863 — The Football Association was formed in England
  2. 1904 — FIFA was founded in Paris
  3. 1930 — First FIFA World Cup took place in Uruguay
  4. 1955 — First European Cup tournament was held

Interesting facts

  • Match Duration: 90 minutes (two 45-minute halves)
  • Players per Team: 11 active players on the pitch
  • Governing Body: FIFA
  • Most Watched Event: FIFA World Cup Final
